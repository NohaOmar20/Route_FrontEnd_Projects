In the animation part: Here is what i used to make it look smooth without using library. 
In the marquee-container: 
1.overflow: hidden;
What it does: Clips (hides) any content that extends outside the container's left or right edges.

Why it's needed: Without this, the extra set of duplicated logos would spill off the screen and cause horizontal scrollbars on your webpage.

2. user-select: none;
What it does: Prevents users from accidentally highlighting/selecting text or images when clicking and dragging over the sliding logos.

3. The Fade Effect (mask-image) This is the key styling detail that makes the ticker look polished:

How mask-image works: In CSS masks, any part of the layer covered by black stays 100% visible (opaque), while any part covered by transparent becomes invisible.

The Gradient breakdown:

0% to 10%: Fades smoothly from transparent (invisible) to black (fully visible) on the far left.

10% to 90%: Remains solid black, keeping all logos in the middle completely visible.

90% to 100%: Fades smoothly back to transparent on the far right.

The Result: Logos don't just "pop" into view or suddenly cut off at the screen edge; they gently fade in on one side and fade out on the other.
